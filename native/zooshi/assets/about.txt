Your floating kitchen brings warm dinners to a city of waterways.
Start at the old pier, deliver 25 meals, and light the night market.
At 100 deliveries, recover a page from Grandma's missing recipe book.
These are story milestones; the new city artwork is still in development.

Choose a 90-second run or the original Classic mode in Options.
